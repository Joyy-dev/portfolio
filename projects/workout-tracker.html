Health & Fitness Companion

Workout Tracker: Data-Driven Habit Building

An offline-first strength training log and progress visualizer optimized for minimal gym friction and rapid entry.

Role

Sole Developer & UI Designer

Tech Stack

Flutter, Hive DB, FL Chart

Architecture

Offline-First, Riverpod

Project Overview

Most workout trackers are bloated with unnecessary social features that slow down athletes during a workout. Workout Tracker was designed specifically for lightning-fast set logging, rest timing, and instant 1-Rep-Max estimation.

Key Highlights

  • Zero Latency Logging: Local embedded Hive database guarantees immediate response even inside basement gym facilities with zero cellular reception.
  • Smart Rest Timer: Audio and haptic alerts that trigger even with the phone locked or running in the background.
  • Volume Progression Charts: Dynamic interactive charts visualizing weekly tonnage and progressive overload across major lifts.
